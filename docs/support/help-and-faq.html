Troubleshooting

Changes are missing on another device

  1. Check the home. Open Family on both devices and confirm that each has joined the same shared home. A profile or a sent invitation alone does not establish sharing.
  2. Check connectivity and status. Connect both devices to the internet. Open More → Privacy & data → Sync & recovery and read the status or error. Keep the app open while it refreshes.
  3. Check Apple access. Confirm iCloud is available on each device and that the owner has sufficient iCloud storage. Ask the owner to check that the participant still has access.
  4. Keep your local records. Do not delete the home, clear data or reinstall to fix a sync problem. Preserve needed records first. If access was removed, use the recovery options shown in the app.

I did not receive an assignment alert

  1. Check the saved assignment. Confirm the item is assigned to the intended member in the shared home. On the receiving device, check the selected personal member and alert preferences.
  2. Check device permissions. In iOS Settings → Notifications → LunaHearth, check notification permission, sounds and delivery settings. Focus and Scheduled Summary can affect alerts.
  3. Check the original item. Open LunaHearth on the receiving device and allow sync to refresh. Confirm the assignment is visible before troubleshooting delivery.
  4. Allow for delivery limits. Background delivery may be delayed or unavailable. Recheck important plans in the app; do not rely on LunaHearth for emergencies or time-critical safety alerts.

A receipt has incorrect or abbreviated items

  1. Improve the image. Use an evenly lit, sharp photo with the receipt flat. Include the store, item lines, date and total. Avoid glare and clipped edges.
  2. Review the draft. Compare item names, amounts, units, prices, currency, discounts and the total with the original. Correct uncertain names manually; optional AI decoding is a suggestion.
  3. Choose destinations. Review expense recording, pantry packs and shopping matches before saving. Do not infer expiry or allergens from a receipt.
  4. Avoid importing twice. If saving seemed interrupted, check Expenses, shopping status and pantry quantity before scanning the receipt again. Keep the original receipt for reference.

My pantry quantity or matching item looks wrong

  1. Check filters and packs. Clear pantry filters and search the ingredient. Separate packs may intentionally have different units, storage locations or dates.
  2. Review the purchase. When buying more, choose the existing ingredient or pack and check the amount and compatible unit. A separate pack creates a separate record.
  3. Correct the saved record. Open the item and adjust the amount or low-stock status to match what is actually at home. The app cannot measure consumption.
  4. Keep distinct ingredients distinct. Review similar-item suggestions. Whole and ground spices, varieties, brands and separately dated packs may need to stay separate. Do not merge solely because AI suggests it.

AI is missing, paused or gives an unexpected answer

  1. Read AI Status. Open More → AI Status to check device capability and availability. Apple Intelligence must be supported and ready; a standard iPhone 15 does not offer these on-device AI actions.
  2. Use the available fallback. Continue with manual entry, supported label reading or built-in ideas. Publisher-paused features may show a message; repeatedly reinstalling will not enable them.
  3. Make the request specific. For dish ideas, include the dish name and intended ingredients. Shorten long requests and review all results. AI can omit a main ingredient or misunderstand a name.
  4. Check the original records. Existing pantry or shopping matches may be excluded from suggestions. Compare results with actual stock and product labels. AI Status records only a result category and time, not proof that an answer is correct.

An invitation opens setup or will not join

  1. Use Apple sharing. The owner should use Family sharing and send the invitation through Apple’s sharing screen. Adding a member name alone is not an invitation.
  2. Open on the intended device. Open the invitation using the recipient’s intended iCloud account. Confirm the address carefully, including dots. Follow the join flow rather than creating a second home for the same household.
  3. Check the invitation. Ask the owner to check the participant and share status. If Apple reports an invalid or revoked invitation, have the owner send a current invitation using the sharing controls.
  4. Preserve existing data. If joining still fails, note the exact error and the app version. Do not clear the existing home to troubleshoot. Do not send invitation links or account passwords to support.

An appointment is missing from Apple Calendar

  1. Check the event option. LunaHearth events do not automatically become Apple Calendar events. Open the event and check its optional Apple Calendar export setting.
  2. Check permission and calendar. Check calendar permission in device Settings and the writable destination calendar chosen in the app.
  3. Check visibility. In Apple Calendar, make sure the destination calendar is shown and the date is correct. Read any pending or error status in LunaHearth.
  4. Understand the direction. LunaHearth → Apple Calendar is an optional export. Do not assume edits made in Apple Calendar update LunaHearth, or that a LunaHearth family share exposes someone’s personal calendars.

What to include when asking for help

  1. Describe the problem. Note the app version/build, iOS version, feature, exact error, expected result and steps that reproduce the issue.
  2. Protect your information. Crop or redact receipts, addresses, family names and financial details in screenshots. Never send passwords, verification codes, private invitation links or a full household backup.
  3. Use a confirmed contact. Use the publisher support contact on the App Store listing. Do not send sensitive records to an unverified email address. This offline guide does not upload diagnostics or send a support request.

Privacy & responsible use

AI, pantry and meal safety

  1. Ideas need review. AI and scanning may produce incorrect, incomplete or unsuitable names, matches, quantities, dates and summaries. Check important details before saving or acting.
  2. Food safety is not assessed. Pantry dates, colours, photographs and meal ideas do not establish freshness, safe storage, safe preparation or absence of allergens. Check actual packaging and authoritative food-safety advice. Do not taste food to test whether it is safe.
  3. Meal ideas are not recipes. LunaHearth provides planning ideas and ingredient lists, not verified cooking instructions or medical, allergy or nutrition advice. Use a trusted recipe and appropriate professional advice for specific dietary needs.
  4. Keep important safeguards separate. Alerts and sync may be delayed. The app is not an emergency, medical or safety-monitoring service. Use appropriate independent reminders for critical obligations.

Privacy, sharing and deletion

  1. Local records and optional sharing. Household records are stored locally. Optional CloudKit backup and sharing use Apple services. Invited participants may access shared information according to their permissions.
  2. Public settings are separate. The app reads publisher feature settings and guidance from public CloudKit. These settings requests do not include household content. This does not mean Apple receives no service information.
  3. On-device intelligence. The app’s AI adapters run on device and do not send household prompts to a third-party AI service. Local AI diagnostics retain a feature, result category and timestamp; they can be cleared in AI Status.
  4. Deletion has a scope. Read the confirmation before clearing data. Live shared records, private backups, exported files and Apple app copies have separate lifecycles. Removing a participant cannot erase copies already received. Read the privacy policy for details.

Important information about using LunaHearth

  1. Purpose and limits. LunaHearth helps organize information you record. It does not inspect your home, measure inventory, verify purchases or guarantee the completeness of household records.
  2. Spending records. Expense reports summarize entered purchases and separate currencies. They are not connected bank balances, audited accounts, tax advice or a substitute for original receipts.
  3. Your choices. Only enter or share information you are entitled to use. Review recipients, permissions, AI proposals and deletion confirmations. Keep separate copies of records you need.
  4. Your rights remain. These explanations describe product limitations; they do not remove mandatory consumer rights or exclude responsibilities that applicable law does not permit a provider to exclude. The applicable App Store licence and privacy policy remain separate documents.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my Apple Reminders stored?

New entries made through the Apple Reminders connection are stored in the selected Apple list. They are separate from shared LunaHearth household records. Manage Apple list sharing and retained entries in Apple Reminders.

Are notifications guaranteed to arrive immediately?

No. Permissions, Focus, connectivity and Apple background scheduling can affect delivery. Check the saved assignment or appointment directly for important plans.

Does the 5 PM reminder generate tomorrow’s meal?

No. It invites you to open meal planning. Choose a style, review ideas and save a plan yourself.

Can a kitchen photo create an exact inventory?

No. A photo can miss, duplicate or misidentify visible items. Review suggested names against the actual products; it cannot establish exact remaining amounts, expiry or food safety.

Why was rice missing from a shopping suggestion?

An ingredient already recorded in pantry or the open shopping list may be filtered out. Check the saved amount and stock status. Add extra quantities manually when needed.

Why are expenses split by currency?

Reports do not convert exchange rates. Choose the month and currency to see the corresponding recorded purchases.

Does deleting the app delete all cloud data?

Do not assume so. CloudKit backups, shared records, system backups and Apple Calendar or Reminders copies can remain. Use the appropriate in-app and Apple data controls and read their confirmation messages.

Does a green date or matching ingredient mean food is safe?

No. Dates and categories are organizational aids. They do not verify freshness, allergens, handling or safe cooking.

Will folding or resizing save or complete anything?

No. Adaptive layouts can rearrange panels while keeping your place. Resizing alone does not buy, complete, delete or save household items. Physical hinge detection is not implemented.

Does this guidance replace the privacy policy or licence?

No. Help explains how features work. Product limitations do not replace the applicable licence, privacy policy or mandatory consumer rights.

Why is something missing on another device?

Check that both devices joined the same home and have internet access. In More → Privacy & data → Sync & recovery, review the sync status. Keep your local records while resolving a sync issue.

Does inventory update itself?

Receipts and shopping purchases can update inventory after your review. The app cannot see what you consume. Update amounts or mark items used up as needed.

What if an item is already in my pantry?

Review the existing match when completing shopping or importing a receipt. Check the amount and unit before updating it. Choose a separate pack only when you want a separate record.

Where are the AI buttons?

AI-only controls appear when Apple's on-device model is available. Check More → AI Status. Manual entry, label reading and built-in ideas remain available without AI.

Can I use the app without sharing?

Yes. Household records work locally. iCloud sharing is optional. Help and setup progress stay on this device and do not turn on services or request permissions.

Can a receipt get a name or total wrong?

Yes. Check every suggested name, amount and total against the receipt and packaging. Optional AI decoding also needs review. You can correct the entries before saving.
