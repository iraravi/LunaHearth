Legal & privacy release review

Draft for review, September 30, 2026. No legal clearance or guarantee of protection is claimed. No new agreement has been published or accepted.

Keep these distinctions clear

In-app responsible-use guidance explains limitations. It is not a custom EULA, an indemnity, a medical disclaimer that makes an unsafe feature acceptable, or a guarantee against a claim. Do not say that users waive all rights or that the publisher has no liability in every circumstance.

Licence

Apple provides a standard app EULA where an applicable custom agreement is not supplied. Confirm the actual App Store Connect licence setting before release. If a custom EULA is needed, obtain jurisdiction-specific review and meet Apple’s minimum requirements; do not silently replace the existing licence.

Privacy policy release blockers

Confirm the legal publisher identity, public support/privacy contact, effective date, public policy URL, support-data handling and retention/deletion practices. Verify App Store privacy answers against the shipped binary and enabled Apple features. Keep the in-app policy labelled draft until these facts are confirmed. Do not invent an address, incorporation status, retention period or legal basis.

Food and AI wording

Keep concise warnings next to receipt, photo and meal review, not only in legal pages. Make clear that names, quantities, dates, totals and suggestions can be wrong. Pantry colours and dates do not certify safety. Do not promise allergen detection or safe meals. Keep cooking instructions outside this feature’s scope and retain manual review before writes.

Consumer rights

Preserve non-excludable consumer rights and statutory responsibilities. Do not insert blanket exclusions or a chosen court/jurisdiction without advice appropriate to the publisher and target markets. India, US and other distribution markets may impose different obligations.

Information sharing and support

Explain that participants can retain copies, that deletion has a defined scope, and that Apple Calendar/Reminders exports are separate. Support should request minimal diagnostic information and never passwords, verification codes, invitation links or full household backups by default.

Before public release

Have an appropriately qualified lawyer review the final licence/privacy wording for the publisher and distribution markets. Review actual food-related features with a qualified food-safety professional if claims or cooking/safety guidance expand. Documentation alone cannot guarantee enforceability or eliminate product risk. This checklist is a review aid, not legal advice.
