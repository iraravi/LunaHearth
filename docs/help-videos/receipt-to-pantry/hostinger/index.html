From receipt to pantry.

Check the amount. Choose what belongs in your pantry. Save when everything looks right.

Silent, with on-screen instructions · Actual app screens with sample data · Visuals may vary by version.

Read the instructions
  1. Start with a clear receipt. Open Home → Expenses → Scan receipt. Photograph the full receipt or choose a photo.
  2. Review the scan. Compare the total and currency with the original receipt. Check and edit item names, prices and quantities before saving. Scanning can miss or misread details.
  3. Choose pantry items. Tap Add all for eligible items, or select individual cards. Use Clear selection to start again. Choose food kept at home; leave meals eaten out, services and gifts unselected.
  4. Review and save. Tap Save receipt to review your choices. Choose Save & update pantry for selected items, or Save without inventory to record spending only.
