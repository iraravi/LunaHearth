Know what is at home.
Open Home, then Pantry. This screen records food you have at home. Search before adding something, so you can check whether the same item is already recorded.

Add the item. Keep it simple.
Choose Add item. Only the item name is required. Add an amount, unit, storage location or pack details if you know them. Use packaging for expiry dates.

Keep your stock accurate.
Open a saved item to update its amount or mark it running low. Use a separate pack when needed. Do not treat a recorded expiry date as proof that food is safe.

Bring purchases back to Pantry.
After shopping, review pantry updates when recording the purchase or receipt. Select only what came home. Check whether to restock an existing pack, and avoid entering the same purchase twice.
