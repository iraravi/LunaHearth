Choose who shares your home.
Open Family from More. You can use the home on your own or enable private iCloud sharing. Review the explanation before uploading. Adding a member profile alone does not grant access.

Invite through Apple sharing.
After enabling sharing, use the invitation controls and Apple sharing screen. The other person opens the invitation with their iCloud account. They should join your home, not create another copy.

Check access and connection.
Review participants and access in the sharing controls. Both devices need connectivity for changes to sync, and updates can take time. Keep using local records when the network is unavailable.

Backups and sync are different.
A saved iCloud backup is a separate snapshot, not the same as live family sync. Open Manage backups under Privacy and data to review backup and restore options before using them.
