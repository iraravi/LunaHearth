See your household plans.
Open Calendar to see household appointments. Use the date and people controls to focus the view. If an appointment seems missing, check the selected date and person filters.

Add the right time and person.
Use the add action to create an appointment. Enter its title, date and time, then choose who it belongs to. Review repeat or reminder options when you need them.

See what is coming today.
After saving, review the appointment in Calendar. Today shows the schedule for the current day. Open the original appointment if you need to edit its details or check the time.

Connect Apple Calendar by choice.
Apple Calendar integration is optional. Use the appointment option when you want a copy there and review the destination calendar. Permission is needed; using the local household calendar does not require it.
