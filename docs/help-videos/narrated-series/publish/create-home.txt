Choose how to begin.
Welcome to Nam Illam. Choose Create a new home if you are starting fresh. If your family invited you, join that existing home instead of creating a duplicate.

Give your home a familiar name.
Enter a home name such as Our Home. This is a label inside the app, not your street address or a login account. Then choose Create my home.

Start with one small task.
Your new home starts empty. Open Pantry to record what you have, Shopping for what you need, or Expenses to record a purchase. The setup activities are optional.

Make it part of your day.
Use Today for your schedule and reminders. Your household works locally without an app account. Family sharing and iCloud are optional, and can be set up later.
