Keep the next task visible.
Open Home, then Reminders. The task screen keeps household to dos together. Use it for jobs such as buying supplies, taking out the bins, or preparing for tomorrow.

Give the task a clear name.
Tap the add button. Enter a short, clear title. Set a due date or assign a household member when needed. Check the details, then save the reminder.

Review what needs attention.
Return to Today to review tasks that are due or overdue. Open a task when you need to make changes. Complete it when the work is actually finished.

Choose Apple integration separately.
Apple Reminders integration is optional. Enable it through Apple connections and review the chosen list and permissions. A local household task is not automatically a reminder in every Apple list.
