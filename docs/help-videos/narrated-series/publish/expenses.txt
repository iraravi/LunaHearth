Keep spending together.
Open Expenses from Home. You can enter a purchase manually or scan a receipt. This is a record of what you enter, not a connection to your bank account.

Check amount and currency.
For manual entry, review the amount, currency, store and purchase date. Check the decimal places carefully. Save after the details agree with the purchase you want to record.

Review every scanned amount.
If you scan, compare the total and currency with the original receipt. Correct misread names, prices or quantities. Keep expense recording selected when you want this purchase included in Expenses.

Read your monthly picture.
Review spending by month and currency. Currencies are kept separate without exchange rate conversion. Pantry updates are a separate choice during receipt review. An expense alone does not automatically stock your pantry.
