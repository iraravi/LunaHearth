Start with your ingredients.
Open Meals from Home. Choose your cooking style and date, then review ideas based on recorded pantry ingredients. Keep pantry amounts current so those ideas have useful context.

Review before you plan.
Check an idea against what you actually have and your dietary needs. Built in ideas work without Apple Intelligence. These are meal ideas, not complete cooking instructions or safety advice.

Fill the gaps with Shopping.
When planning a meal, review missing ingredients and add what you need to Shopping. Check existing shopping matches first. Buy the ingredients, then review whether the purchase should update Pantry.

Plan tomorrow when it suits you.
You can use the optional five p.m. planning reminder to think about tomorrow. It does not generate or save a meal automatically. You stay in control of the plan.
