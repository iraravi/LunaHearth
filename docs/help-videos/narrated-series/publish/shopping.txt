List what you need.
Open Shopping from Home. Add the things you need to buy. Before adding another entry, check existing items and filters so that something already listed is not duplicated.

Add useful details.
Enter the name. Add an amount, unit, store or buyer when useful. Choose when it is needed if you want to plan ahead. Review the entry, then save.

Choose how to finish.
When you buy an item, tap its purchase circle and review what happens next. You can update pantry inventory, use a separate pack, or finish without adding stock.

Record it only once.
If you scan the receipt after recording a purchase, review recent matches and link the existing purchase when offered. This avoids stocking the same purchase twice. Check the final review before saving.
