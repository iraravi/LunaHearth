Step inside a new way to see your home.

• Explore an interactive home scene with shortcuts to your kitchen, tasks and shopping.
• See your saved groceries in virtual fridge and pantry views. Enjoy opening-door animations, gently drifting fridge mist and a quick switch back to your regular list.
• On supported foldable devices, the home and kitchen doors respond as you open and close your device.
• Improved receipt scanning, including repeated Costco items, discounts, totals and item-name matching.
• Clearer purchase review and pantry choices, with refinements to navigation and appearance.

Your existing household data stays in place when you update. Core features continue to work offline.
