LUNAHEARTH · PRODUCT GUIDE · 30 SEPTEMBER 2026

A calmer way to run your home.

Product scope, everyday workflows and practical limits.

Help, FAQ & troubleshooting

What LunaHearth does

A local-first household organizer for people living alone or together. Keep shopping, pantry, reviewed purchases, meal plans, household appointments, rooms and belongings connected. There is no separate LunaHearth login. Sharing is optional and uses Apple iCloud identity.

Start with one useful workflow

Create your home, add a few actual pantry items, and create a shopping list. Names are enough to start; record quantities, dates and storage only when known. Use the setup checklist and the question-mark help button instead of enabling every service at once.

Find your way around

Today highlights saved priorities and appointments. Calendar holds household scheduling. Home opens your kitchen and home records, including Pantry, Shopping, Meals, Expenses and Rooms & belongings. More contains family sharing, Apple connections, privacy/data controls, AI Status and Help & guides. Availability may vary with device capability and publisher feature settings.

The connected purchase flow

Receipt photo → review extracted fields → choose expense, pantry and shopping changes → save. Review the store, date, currency and total; correct abbreviated item names. Choose existing packs or separate packs and check amounts and units. A receipt does not establish an expiry date. Then check the resulting expense, pantry quantity and shopping completion.

Shopping and pantry

Shopping is what you intend to buy; pantry is a record of what is at home. Completing shopping offers inventory choices, including finishing without adding to pantry. Keep separately dated packs separate when needed. Use compatible units when restocking. Track consumption manually: the app cannot know that a product was used or discarded.

Meals and planning

Choose a cooking style and optionally add context. Review ideas against actual ingredients, packaging and dietary needs. Save a chosen plan and review missing shopping items. Ideas are not verified recipes or cooking instructions. The optional 5 PM alert prompts planning; it does not automatically generate or save a meal.

Expenses and reports

Add an expense manually or record a reviewed receipt. View spending by month and currency. Totals represent recorded purchases, not bank activity. Currencies are not converted. Keep original receipts for tax, reimbursement or other important records.

Household collaboration

The owner invites people through Apple sharing. Recipients join the shared home using their intended iCloud account. A member profile alone does not grant access. Changes are saved locally and optional cloud sync follows; connectivity, storage and account status affect delivery. Review participant permissions and share only information you are entitled to use.

Apple apps and notifications

Apple Calendar export is optional and one-way from LunaHearth; do not assume edits in Apple Calendar return to the household event. New Apple Reminders entries belong to the selected Apple list, separately from LunaHearth household records. Apple services have their own sharing and deletion rules. Notifications are optional per device and may be delayed; they are not an emergency service.

Privacy and on-device AI

Household records live locally with optional CloudKit backup/sharing. Public CloudKit configuration contains publisher settings and guidance, separate from private household content. AI and receipt recognition run on device in current adapters. AI may be unavailable, paused, wrong or incomplete; manual paths and built-in suggestions preserve core use. Publisher support communications and Apple service processing have separate privacy implications.

Accessibility and adaptive layouts

The interface supports Dynamic Type, labelled controls and Reduce Motion. Today can rearrange attention and schedule panels as space changes. This is window-size adaptation, not physical hinge detection. No fold or rotation gesture completes or deletes data.

Data lifecycle

Live records, private backup snapshots, saved documents, exported files and Apple app copies are separate. Read each deletion confirmation and preserve copies you need. Uninstalling the app or disabling a connection does not prove all remote copies are deleted. Removing access cannot retract copies another participant already saved.

Known limits and release status

This guide describes TestFlight build 66. Public App Store release is being prepared for India and is not yet available. Actual AI quality, notification timing, physical folding and full assistive-technology behaviour need device validation. No claim of certification, guaranteed data recovery, allergy safety or legal compliance is made.
