DESCRIPTION
A little order. More room for living.

LunaHearth brings everyday household planning into one place on iPhone and iPad.

YOUR DAY, IN FOCUS
See household appointments, reminders, shopping and pantry items that need attention.

A CONNECTED KITCHEN
Keep a shopping list with stores, quantities and assigned buyers. When you buy something, choose whether to stock your pantry. Track amounts, low stock and the expiry dates you enter.

RECEIPTS AND SPENDING
Scan a receipt, review the extracted details and record a purchase. Keep monthly expense totals alongside your household records.

PLAN WHAT TO COOK
Explore pantry-based meal ideas and review a weekly meal plan. Add missing ingredients to shopping and check what is already on your list.

YOUR HOME, ORGANIZED
Keep rooms, belongings, maintenance and warranty details together. Start manually and explore optional room-layout tools when useful.

OPTIONAL APPLE CONNECTIONS
Share your home through iCloud. Choose which appointments to send to Apple Calendar, and connect selected Apple Reminders lists if you want to.

LOCAL FIRST
Core household records work offline without a LunaHearth login. On-device intelligence is an optional enhancement; availability depends on the device, OS and Apple Intelligence settings. Review extracted receipt and photo details before saving. Pantry photo recognition uses supported on-device image capabilities, with readable-label scanning and manual entry as fallbacks.

iCloud sharing needs compatible Apple accounts and available iCloud storage. Expiry dates are user-entered records, not a food-safety assessment.

Meal and AI suggestions can be incomplete or incorrect. Check ingredient labels, allergens and preparation instructions. The app does not assess food safety or provide medical or dietary advice. Expense totals reflect the records you save; verify them against original receipts. Sync and notifications may be delayed.

WHATS_NEW
Initial public release: household planning, shopping, pantry, meals, receipt review, expenses and optional Apple integrations.
