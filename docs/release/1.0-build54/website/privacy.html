LunaHearth

Privacy policy — draft

Not published. Confirm publisher details and support contact before use.

Your household information

LunaHearth stores household records on your device. These may include member display names, schedules, reminders, shopping, pantry contents, meal plans, expenses, rooms, belongings and documents that you choose to enter. The app does not require a LunaHearth account.

Optional iCloud

If you enable iCloud backup or household sharing, data is sent to Apple CloudKit using your Apple account. Shared household records are available to participants you invite through Apple sharing. The owner’s iCloud storage and Apple’s service terms apply. Participants may save or copy information they can access. Removing access does not erase copies already received. Sync requires connectivity and can be delayed.

Photos, receipts and on-device intelligence

Camera and selected photos are used when you choose a scanning/import feature. Receipt text recognition and optional generative assistance run on the device. Pantry scan photos are transient in the scan workflow; confirmed item details are saved. Imported documents may be retained when you explicitly save them. The app does not send household prompts to a third-party AI service. Review extracted information before saving.

Apple connections and notifications

Calendar and Reminders access is optional and requested when connecting. Selected information may be read or written to Apple services according to your choices. Device notifications support reminders and household updates. Permissions can be changed in device Settings.

Tracking

No advertising, attribution, third-party analytics or tracking SDK is included in the audited app. Apple may process service and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Your controls

More > Privacy & data provides data controls and private backup management. Review each deletion confirmation carefully. Live household data, backups, saved files and copies exported to Apple apps can have separate lifecycles; deleting one does not imply every separate copy is erased. Participants may retain previously received information. Keep an export or backup you need before clearing records.

Device backups and external copies

System device backups may contain app data according to your Apple settings, separately from the app’s optional CloudKit features. Turning off a connection does not itself delete previously saved cloud data or entries in Apple Calendar or Reminders. Manage these copies in the relevant service.

People in your home

Only enter and share information you are entitled to use. Tell household members what you share and check participant access before adding personal details. Member profiles are household records, not separate authenticated app accounts.

Support and changes

Publisher support contact and retention practices must be supplied before publishing this draft. If you contact support, avoid sending sensitive household information unless it is necessary. The publisher must confirm the final effective date and how policy changes will be communicated.
