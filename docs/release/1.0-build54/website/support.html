Start with your home

Create a home, then use Home to open Pantry, Shopping, Meals, Expenses or Rooms. The setup guide helps you review each step. You can use core features without connecting iCloud.

Shopping and pantry

Add the item name first; other details are optional. Tap a shopping item when bought and choose whether to add it to inventory. Review matching stock, quantity units and expiry dates rather than creating unintended duplicates.

Sharing

Use More > Family and Apple’s sharing panel. The recipient should open the invitation on the intended iCloud account. Data sync needs connectivity and available owner iCloud storage. Preserve local data if a share becomes unavailable.

Scanning and AI

Check More > AI Status to see current device capability. Unclear receipt names and kitchen labels may need manual correction. AI is optional and its output must be reviewed.

Review suggestions and records

AI and scan results can be wrong or incomplete. Check names, quantities, dates and receipt totals before saving. Meal suggestions do not verify allergens or food safety; check ingredient labels and appropriate preparation guidance. Expense reports summarize saved records and are not financial or tax advice. Notifications and syncing can be delayed; check important dates directly.

Something is not working

Keep the app installed to preserve local records. Note the app version, device and steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords, Apple verification codes, or unredacted household documents to support.
