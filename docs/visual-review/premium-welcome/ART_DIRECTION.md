# A considered home — welcome and setup direction

September 16, 2026. Requested direction: the restraint and finish of a five-star hotel or premium cabin; remove the bright blue, crowded, cartoon-like introduction.

## Implemented

Ivory/charcoal adaptive surfaces, muted sage details, regular serif headlines, compact primary actions, quiet underlined chapter navigation and shorter copy. No colorful tile grid, large plastic icons, dark photo gradient, looping highlight or blue sofa. The introduction, home creation and existing setup checklist share these tokens. More uses the new residence photograph. Dark mode and Dynamic Type remain native, and large accessibility text prioritizes instructions over decorative images.

Only the selected chapter underline animates, disabled for Reduce Motion. Text changes immediately to avoid overlapping headings at large sizes. Progress, optional AI/review warnings, offline behavior, invitation priority, and setup completion semantics are unchanged. This is a redesign of the welcome/setup journey, not a claim that every feature screen has been redesigned.

## Final prompts

### Residence
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production landscape editorial photograph for the welcome of a premium private home-management iOS app. Create a single beautiful realistic photograph, landscape 3:2 composition, of a serene contemporary home dining nook and kitchen, with the restrained elegance and impeccable detailing of a five-star boutique hotel suite. Pale limestone island, warm ivory plaster, soft linen curtain, subtly fluted natural oak, one sculptural off-white ceramic vase with a small olive branch, a simple bowl with two lemons on stone. Airy soft morning daylight, refined natural textures, understated architectural photography, balanced spacious composition, neutral whites and soft stone with natural muted green and small fresh lemon yellow accents. No blue furniture, no cartoon, no miniature dollhouse, no toy-like 3D, no glossy plastic, no gold glamour, no brown or sepia cast, no clutter, no humans, no logos, no text. Actual interior editorial photograph, sophisticated and lived-in but beautifully tidy. Main composition must crop gracefully as a horizontal banner or portrait on iPad.

### Table
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape editorial food still life for a premium private home-management app welcome guide. One sophisticated realistic photograph, landscape 3:2. A beautifully restrained still life on pale ivory limestone in soft natural daylight: a small shallow handmade ivory bowl with three fresh pears, one glass carafe of water, a folded natural off-white linen napkin and a very small olive sprig, with faint out-of-focus bright contemporary kitchen architecture in background. Composition airy, sculptural, tactile, perfectly considered like a five-star hotel breakfast welcome, almost magazine architectural still-life photography. Neutral true whites, pale stone, fresh natural pear green; no brown/sepia grading, no blue, no saturated blocks, no basket, no plastic, no cartoon, no 3D toy look, no shiny gold, no clutter, no branding, no text, no human. It must feel expensive through restraint and beautiful real materials, not excessive decoration. Objects clustered centrally with negative space; usable for wide banner and portrait crops.
